Privacy Policy for Openspeak

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Openspeak ("the App", "we", "us") is a translation app developed by Zulkarnaine Teli. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information. By using Openspeak, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Openspeak is designed to minimize data collection. Here is exactly what the App accesses:

Data Type What It's For Where It's Stored
Anonymous device identifier Each installation is assigned an anonymous, random ID via Firebase Anonymous Authentication. No name, email, phone number, or other personal identity is collected or linked to this ID. Firebase (Google) servers
Microphone audio Used only when you tap the microphone button, to convert your speech into text for translation. Audio is processed for this purpose and is not stored or used for any other purpose. Processed in-app; not retained after conversion
Camera images Used only when you use the camera/OCR feature, to scan and extract text from a photo for translation. Images are processed for text recognition and are not uploaded, stored, or shared beyond this purpose. Processed in-app; not retained after text extraction
Text you enter or scan for translation Sent to translation service providers (see "Third-Party Services" below) solely to generate a translation. Processed by third-party translation APIs; see below
Translation history (source text, translated text, timestamps, favorites) Saved locally on your device so you can revisit past translations. Stored only on your device (local database). Not uploaded to any server.

Third-Party Services

To provide translations, Openspeak sends the text you want translated to one or more of the following third-party services. These providers process the text solely to return a translation result and are bound by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Gemini API — used for AI-powered translation when available.
  • Google ML Kit (on-device) — used for offline translation; this processing happens entirely on your device and is not sent to any server.
  • Other translation service providers (e.g. free translation APIs) may be used as fallback options to ensure translation is available even when other services are unreachable.

We do not control how these third parties process data beyond the translation request itself. We recommend reviewing Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy for details on how Google-operated services handle data.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not require you to create an account or log in.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or ad targeting.

How You Can Control or Delete Your Data

  • You can delete individual translations, or clear your entire translation history at any time, directly within the App (Recent tab → Clear History).
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data (translation history) from your device.
  • Because we do not collect personal identity information, there is no separate account to delete.

Children's Privacy

Openspeak is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us at openspeak.support@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps to address it.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App, including restricting API access and using secure connections (HTTPS) for network requests. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
